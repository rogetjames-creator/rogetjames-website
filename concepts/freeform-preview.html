FreeformBeyond the rectangle — laser cut metal wall art & screens

The artwork draws its own edge.

A conventional screen begins with a rectangle and fills it with pattern. Freeform begins with the composition — a banksia, a creeping fig, a branch — and lets that form decide the outline. No frame, no border. The wall behind becomes part of the work, and the shadow it casts is its second drawing.

Every piece in the ROGETjames Wall Art collection is Freeform. Each is designed by James Roget and cut in Corten steel or powder-coated aluminium, to suit architecture, interiors and landscape.

Screen

The architecture holds the pattern. A panel sized to an opening — fence, gate, divider, privacy.

Freeform

The artwork sets its own geometry. The silhouette is the design, and the wall is its ground.
