Beyond the Rectangle — positioning Freeform and strengthening the Screens offer

Aim. Establish Freeform as a signature ROGETjames design approach — the artwork sets its own outline rather than sitting inside a rectangular panel — and build the independent evidence that lets search engines and AI assistants recommend ROGETjames on merit.

Ideas

  1. Wall Art is Freeform. Every piece in the Wall Art collection is Freeform — the artwork sets its own outline. That gives Freeform a large, established body of work from day one. The story: Screens (a pattern inside a rectangle) → Freeform (the whole Wall Art collection, and screens that break the rectangle).
  2. A curated Freeform page at rogetjames.com/freeform, drawing on the entire Wall Art collection plus Freeform screens and built installations. No new shoot needed to launch. No prices shown.
  3. “From Q DESIGN to ROGETjames” timeline — the history of the Wall Art collection is the history of Freeform. Only pieces with a real date and photograph. No claims of being first; the evidence makes the point.
  4. “Beyond the Rectangle” editorial — written once, published on the site first, then pitched to architecture and design publications so editors have something to cite.
  5. Three screen case studies, each its own page: photographs, location, design name, application, material, architect or builder credit, and a link to the design. This answers the main gap: independent proof.
  6. A Freeform option in the photo uploader, so new work tagged Freeform appears on the page automatically.
  7. Cross-links between Screens, Freeform and Wall Art (and the footer), and “Freeform” wording on the Wall Art page itself, so visitors and search engines connect the two.
  8. Monthly AI-recommendation check — neutral questions that don’t name ROGETjames, asked of ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Perplexity, recorded over time.

Guardrails

  • No claims of invention or being first — other suppliers also use the term “Freeform Screens”.
  • No competitor ranking in public material; the earlier ranking is provisional.
  • No prices on the Freeform page — pricing stays behind the postcode step.
  • Existing Wall Art and Screens collections remain unchanged.

Measures of success

  • ROGETjames named, unprompted, in neutral AI recommendations
  • Independent articles and credits citing ROGETjames as designer
  • Visibility for non-branded searches such as “freeform metal wall art” and “architectural laser cut screens”
  • Enquiries from architects, landscape architects and designers

Critical missing fact: the earliest documented Wall Art (Freeform) piece and its date — this sets how strongly the history can be told.
